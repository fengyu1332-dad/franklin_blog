   Where raw sparks become forged products

    Everyone has ideas. Few can turn them into something real. IdeaForge (AI灵感锻造炉) is a full-stack AI workshop that
    walks anyone — no business training, no technical degree required — through the entire journey from a vague spark to a
     complete, investable product plan.

    ---
    From "what if" to "here it is" — a four-stage forge

    ① Sensing the real need.
    Before a single solution is drawn, IdeaForge listens. It applies five rigorously engineered innovation methodologies —
     TRIZ, JTBD, First Principles, Lateral Thinking, and SCAMPER — each with an advanced professional edition, to strip
    away surface desires and expose the true, unmet need beneath. It scans existing products, names their limits, and
    opens three or more defensible innovation directions.

    ② Forging the first concept.
    Your chosen direction is forged into a structured initial proposal: product positioning, core features, market
    opportunity, technical path, and a go-to-market angle — clear enough to argue about, solid enough to build on.

    ③ The council of five experts.
    The proposal is placed before five uncompromising specialists: the Product Architect, the Market Analyst, the Tech
    Lead, the UX/UI Director, and the Growth Hacker. Each delivers blunt, actionable critique — not flattery. You decide:
    accept, edit, or reject each point, and set its priority.

    ④ The final synthesis.
    Only the opinions you chose are melted back into the furnace. Every accepted point is answered in a complete Business
    Plan & Requirements Document — not vaguely, but as concrete design, architecture, and strategy. Where expert opinions
    irreconcilably clash, the tool says so honestly, explains the trade-off, and if an idea truly cannot be forged, it
    says so too. Honesty is not a failure mode here; it is the standard.

    Progress is tracked from 0% to 100%, every project is saved and restorable, and the final document exports cleanly to
    Markdown.

    ---
    The philosophy behind the forge

    IdeaForge was not built to make its creator a little smarter. It was built by a believer in technology who carries a
    deeply human conviction: give a person a fish and they eat for a day; teach a person to fish and they eat for a
    lifetime.

    The real "fish" here is not any single product — it is the method. When a novice relies on a wizard who hands them the
     answer, they remain a novice. When they are guided, step by step, through the thinking of a TRIZ master or a JTBD
    practitioner, they slowly inherit a repeatable habit of rigorous innovation. The tool never hides its reasoning; it
    shows its work, so that every user — student, founder, tinkerer — is not just receiving an answer, but quietly
    learning to forge their own.

    This is infrastructure for generosity: a forge that turns everyone's intuition into everyone's habit. The goal is not
    one brilliant mind. The goal is a world where innovation is no longer a rare gift, but a common habit.
