title: Major Star Map (专业星图) — A Free Career Guidance Platform for Students
description: >-
  Major Star Map is a public-interest educational platform built to help high
  school and college students understand
    their academic and career future. Driven by a simple mission — every student deserves clear, honest guidance when
    choosing a major — the site offers all of its core services completely free of charge.

    What we offer:

    - Complete major database: In-depth profiles for all 883 undergraduate majors under the Ministry of Education's 2026
    catalog, covering curriculum, learning difficulty, salary ranges, recommended universities, and career directions.
    - AI deep-analysis reports: Each major comes with a comprehensive report (career outlook, salary trends, graduate
    school and civil service prospects, industry growth, AI impact, and a candid "straight-talk" review), generated by our
     AI to give students data-backed insights instead of vague advice.
    - Professional aptitude assessment: An interactive assessment that helps students discover which majors fit their
    interests, abilities, and personality.
    - Major comparison & career mapping: Tools to compare majors side by side and visualize career pathways across
    industries.
    - AI career-planning consultant: A specially built AI career-planning assistant — powered by our own skill system —
    that answers students' questions about majors and career development free of charge, at any time.

    Our goal is not commercial. It is to level the playing field: whether a student has access to expensive advisors or
    not, everyone can get honest, professional, and free guidance about the road ahead.
